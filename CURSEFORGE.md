# My Compagnon

**My Compagnon** is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that gives you a survival companion capable of following you, defending you and helping with everyday tasks.

Instead of behaving like a simple custom mob, the companion is based on Minecraft's simulated-player capabilities. This lets it interact with the world in a player-like way: it can move, fight, use items, manage equipment, pick up drops, eat, sleep and work with blocks and containers.

## Main features

- Companion that follows and protects its owner.
- Attacks hostile mobs and targets attacked by the owner.
- Automatic armor, tool, offhand and hotbar management.
- Hunger, healing and sleep management.
- Creeper protection when a shield is available.
- Item pickup and inventory management.
- Crop-farming mode with selectable farm area.
- Automatic planting and harvesting of mature crops.
- Chest selection for crop storage and inventory unloading.
- Persistent companion data between play sessions.
- In-game menu for companion behavior and custom naming.
- French and English localization.

## Staff of Authority

The **Staff of Authority** is the main interaction tool.

Use it on your companion to open its menu. In crop-farming mode, use it on two blocks to define the farm area and directly on a chest to choose the storage chest. Outside crop-farming mode, using the staff on a chest can order the companion to empty its inventory into that chest.

### Version 1.1.1 fix

Version 1.1.1 fixes chest targeting. The staff now uses the exact block clicked by the player instead of relying on a view-direction block raycast, preventing the block below or behind a chest from being selected by mistake.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.30 or newer.
- Script modules enabled for the world.

## Development status

The addon is under active development. New companion behaviors and quality-of-life improvements are planned, and bug reports or suggestions are welcome.
